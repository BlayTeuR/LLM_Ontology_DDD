# Examples

Reference inputs and outputs of the pipeline.

| Folder | Content |
|---|---|
| `insurance/` | `insurance.cml`: the Insurance example from [Context Mapper Insurance example](https://github.com/ContextMapper/context-mapper-examples/tree/master/src/main/cml/insurance-example). `insurance.ttl`: its OWL artifact, generated by the CML to OWL converter (metamodel 0.2.0). |

`insurance.ttl` is versioned on purpose: the reasoner (`reasoner/`) and the Neo4j loader (`neo4j_knowledge_graph/`) use it as a ready-to-use sample, without running the converter. It is a snapshot: after a change to the converter or the metamodel, regenerate it with

```
python cml_to_ttl/src/main_cml_to_ttl.py examples/insurance/insurance.cml
```

then copy `cml_to_ttl/output_cml/ttl/insurance.ttl` to `examples/insurance/insurance.ttl`.

The converter test inputs (with their expected counts) are not examples: they stay in `cml_to_ttl/cml_tests/`.

## References

The `insurance.cml` file is the Insurance example distributed with Context Mapper. The DDD metamodel used by the converter (`metamodel/ddd-metamodel.ttl`) is derived from the strategic and tactical DDD metamodel proposed by Kapferer & Zimmermann (2020) and formalized in OWL 2 DL and SHACL.
